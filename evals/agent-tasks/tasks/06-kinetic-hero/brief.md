Kinetic typography for a product launch: four words slam in one after
another, then a hero word fills the frame.

- Canvas 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 150 frames (5 s), flat background `#0a0a0a`,
  all type in flat `#fafafa`, bold.
- `MOVE`, `FAST`, `BREAK`, `THINGS` slam in at frames 0, 15, 30 and 45 —
  each scales down from big to its resting size in a few frames, and each
  replaces the one before. All four are gone by frame 75.
- From frame 60 the hero word `BUILD` grows in and by frame 90 it fills the
  frame: its capital letters at least 340 px tall, the word still inside the
  frame. It holds, alone on screen, to the end.
- The hero word is the shot people will pause on: at its final size it must
  be as crisp as type set at that size — no soft, upscaled-looking edges.
- Names (`debugName`s): `word-1` … `word-4` for the four words in order, and
  `hero`.
