An image wall for a summer recap: twelve photos in a grid, the camera
slowly pushing in, a title over it.

- Canvas 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 180 frames (6 s).
- `photo-01.png` … `photo-12.png` are 800 × 600. Lay them out as a 4 × 3
  grid that fills the frame edge to edge at frame 0: each photo 480 × 360,
  shown whole (not cropped), no gaps, `photo-01` top-left, in order row by
  row.
- Over the whole film the wall scales from 1.0 to 1.6 about the frame
  centre, eased.
- The title `Summer 2026` fades in over frames 30–45, centred, and holds.
- The photos contain fine texture (fabric, foliage). The wall must not
  shimmer or show moiré as it moves — the client has rejected cuts for that
  before.
- Names (`debugName`s): `wall` for the grid, `photo-1` … `photo-12` for the
  photos, `title`.
